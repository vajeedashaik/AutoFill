Digital Catalyst - AI Engineer
Build intelligent solutions. Scale enterprise impact.
About Tech Mahindra
Tech Mahindra is a leading provider of digital transformation, consulting, and business re-engineering services
and solutions. With a presence in over 90 countries, helping 1100+ global customers, including Fortune 500
companies. We are part of the Mahindra Group, one of the largest business conglomerates in India. Our
mission is to help clients drive innovation, enhance efficiency, and deliver superior customer experience. With
our promise to help our customers ‘scale at speed’, we design future state solutions for industry leaders and
offer innovative digital experiences that enable them to transform and transition.
Your opportunity to build what is next
At Tech Mahindra, AI Engineers do more than train models. They turn ambitious ideas into reliable, responsible
and scalable solutions for global enterprises. You will work with experienced technologists, domain experts
and cross-functional teams to solve real business problems across industries, while learning how productiongrade AI is designed, tested, deployed and improved.
If you enjoy coding, experimenting, questioning assumptions and converting data into decisions, this is
where your AI journey can accelerate.
About the Role
You will be working closely with the internal business units of Tech Mahindra on high-impact client projects,
wherein you will contribute towards pioneering research, coding, developing rapid applications/platforms and
building technology enabled solutions.
AI Solution Development
• Implement machine learning and AI models as per solution designs
• Support development of AI-driven features for applications and platforms
• Convert analytical models into production-ready components
Model Integration & Deployment
• Integrate trained models with APIs, applications, and data pipelines
• Support deployment of AI models on cloud or on-prem platforms
• Assist in monitoring model performance and basic drift indicators
Data & Pipeline Support
• Work with data scientists and data engineers to support feature pipelines
• Perform data preprocessing and transformation for AI use cases
• Ensure data compatibility and reliability for model execution

Testing & Optimization
• Conduct functional testing of AI components and services
• Support model optimization for performance, scalability, and latency
• Assist in debugging issues related to inference and integrations
Collaboration & Delivery
• Collaborate with cross-functional teams including data science, engineering, and QA
• Maintain documentation for AI components, workflows, and integrations
• Adhere to AI engineering standards, security guidelines, and delivery timelines
LOCATION PAN India
